GOOD TO KNOW FAQ

Questions?

Still wondering about something? Send us a note ↗

Who can join?

Middle school runners in Grades 6–8 preparing for high school track and cross country.

Where is the camp?

At DV Track in the Dougherty Valley High School area of San Ramon, California. The exact address will be confirmed before publishing.

When does it start?

Dates and times are still being finalized. Join the interest list for updates.

How much does it cost?

Pricing details are coming soon. Join the interest list or contact us for enrollment details.

How do I secure a spot?

Complete the interest form, then follow the enrollment steps provided by the camp team. Submitting the form alone does not confirm enrollment.

Is this for beginners or experienced runners?

The camp welcomes motivated middle school runners. The camp team can confirm final placement and details.
